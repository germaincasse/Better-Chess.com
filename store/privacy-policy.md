# Better Chess.com Free: privacy policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

Better Chess.com Free is a browser extension for chess.com. This policy explains what data it handles.

## What the extension reads

- On chess.com game pages: the usernames of the two players, and whether the game is finished.
- From chess.com: the moves of a finished game (the same data the chess.com game page loads), and the public statistics of the two players (ratings and peak ratings) from the public chess.com API.
- Your chess.com username, detected from the page or typed in the settings, to know which side you played.

## Where the data goes

- All analysis runs locally in your browser (Stockfish, bundled with the extension).
- Settings, the history of analyzed games and the analysis reports are stored in your browser's extension storage (`chrome.storage.local`). They never leave your device.
- The only network requests are made to chess.com (`www.chess.com` and `api.chess.com`).
- The extension has no server, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Nothing is sent to the developer or to any third party.

## Deleting your data

Use "Clear history" in the extension popup, or remove the extension: Chrome deletes its storage.
